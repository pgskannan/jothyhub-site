JothyHub

A private, self-hosted assistant for one family.

JothyHub runs on a family's own computers at home. It helps family members with reminders, shopping lists, notes, and personal finance tracking, in English and Tamil. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

Gmail access

A family member may choose to connect their own Gmail account. JothyHub then reads that person's mail to notice bills, statements, security warnings, and deliveries, and tells only that person. Connecting is optional and can be undone at any time.

  • Mail is processed on the family's own home computers, not by any cloud AI service.
  • JothyHub never sends, deletes, or changes email.
  • JothyHub never opens links in emails.
