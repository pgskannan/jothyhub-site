JothyHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

JothyHub is a private, self-hosted assistant used by the members of one family. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Who can use it

Only family members who have been given an account. Each person connects their own Google account, by their own choice.

What Google data is accessed

With a person's permission, JothyHub reads that person's Gmail messages (read-only access). In a later version, it may also save draft replies in that person's own Gmail Drafts folder, only after that person grants this separately.

How the data is used

  • To notice items that need attention, such as bills, account statements, security warnings, deliveries, and appointments, and to tell only that person.
  • To save bank and card statement attachments to that person's private folder on the family's home storage, for their own budgeting.

JothyHub never sends email, deletes email, changes labels, clicks links, or acts on anything an email asks it to do.

Where the data is processed and stored

  • All processing happens on the family's own computers at home. Email content is not sent to any cloud AI service or third party.
  • JothyHub stores short facts (for example: sender domain, date, type, amount due, due date), not whole emails. Email text is kept only briefly while it is being processed and is then deleted.
  • Each person's data is kept separately and is not visible to other family members.
  • Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Limited Use

JothyHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train AI or machine-learning models.

Your choices

  • Disconnect at any time from your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps & services), or by asking JothyHub to disconnect.
  • Ask JothyHub to export or delete everything it has stored about you.
